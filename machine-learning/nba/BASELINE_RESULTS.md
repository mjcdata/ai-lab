## Limitations and review

Season-scoped results are not career comparisons. Minutes retain accepted whole-minute precision. No extra exposure threshold is applied: small samples may have unstable rates. All six features require complete observed coverage; missingness is never zero-filled. The scaler includes one latest eligible checkpoint per player, including the query player, and is fixed across queries. Shooting efficiency, position and defense beyond steals/blocks are omitted. Differences are explanations of distance, not causal claims. Review these outputs under WBS 4.1–4.4 before application development; full historical acquisition remains open.
